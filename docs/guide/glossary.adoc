= Glossary

Terms as Protoctist uses them.

Belief (`BELIEF`)::
A claim the gates support, but below the factive threshold at this rank.

Clade cumulus::
The cumulative read count of a clade: the node's own reads plus those of every
descendant. `clade_cumulus(run)` returns it for every node, largest first.

Collapsed (`COLLAPSED`)::
The claim was made at a coarser rank than the one requested.

Contaminated::
A `ResidualInfo` flag, set when a contamination gate fired for the row.

Evidence receipt (`EvidenceReceipt`)::
The record of what a claim rested on: its standpoint, warrant, projection and
an opaque signature. Protoctist carries the receipts it is given. It never
creates one and does not check signatures. Signing and verification stay with
the library that issued the receipt, such as EpistemicTypes.jl.

Factive (`FACTIVE`)::
A claim warranted at this rank by the gates that issued it.

Judgement surface::
The fields `validate_merge` compares: the reference database, its version, the
rank ladder and the classification policy. Two runs that agree on all of them
were judged under the same conditions and can be pooled.

Ladder::
The ordered list of ranks a lineage is read against: `:pr2` (9 ranks) or
`:silva` (7). See link:lineage-formats.adoc[Lineage formats].

Lineage::
A path from the root of a taxonomy to one taxon, written as a separated string
and held as a `TaxPath`.

Like weight ratio (LWR)::
In jplace, a placement's share of the total likelihood across the candidate
edges for its query. `best_placements` selects by it.

Novel (`novel_ok`)::
A `ResidualInfo` flag: the novelty gates were satisfied. It is only ever set
after the contamination gates pass, so a contaminated row is never reported as
novel.

Residual (`ResidualInfo`)::
What is left over after a claim is made: the number of named species still in
contention, the novel and contaminated flags, and a free-text note.

Sans fibre (`SANS_FIBRE`)::
No origin witness. The claim carries no receipt at all.

Standpoint::
Where a claim is made from: the reference, the rank ladder and its version.
Two claims are comparable only from the same standpoint.

Unfilled rank::
A rank inside a lineage with an empty label (`a;;c`). The lineage continues
past it, and the next label keeps its own rank.
