[[estate]]
= What the formal work adds

== Why a protist toolkit carries type theory

[.lead]
Three questions come up in every amplicon study and are usually answered in a
footnote. Is this taxon present, or only not ruled out? What was lost between
the read and the figure? May these two runs be compared? The formal
developments below answer each as data that travels with the result.

The catalogue entries refer to these by name. This part says what each one is,
what has been proved, and which entries use it. Status is quoted from each
repository's own proof ledger as read on 18 November 2026.

== Evidence types

[cols="2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
| Development | What it establishes | Where the catalogue uses it

| *epistemic-types* (Agda)
| A _factive_ modality has a reflection principle: from the evidence one may
  conclude the claim. A _belief_ modality deliberately has none. A token alone
  never yields the claim (`no-receipt-reflection`).
| The four statuses on every row and node: `FACTIVE`, `BELIEF`, `COLLAPSED`,
  `SANS_FIBRE`. Every entry that turns a number into a verdict says which
  status the verdict may carry (placement, detection, pruned search).

| *residual-evidence-types* (Agda) and *ResidualEvidenceTypes.jl*
| Presence and identification come apart. A quantity can be known to be
  nonzero without its value being known. Claims are decided over _all_
  candidate worlds consistent with the observation and the assumptions:
  entailed, refuted, or unresolved, with a witness each way.
| Unassigned and partially assigned reads (`LineageResidual`), detection above
  a noise bound, novel-lineage admissibility, contamination.

| *echo-types* (Agda, with Rocq and Lean legs)
| What a lossy map retains about its input, and when the input can be
  recovered from what was retained.
| Round trips: lineage string to `TaxPath` and back, jplace read and write,
  rank truncation, table import and export.

| *EpistemicTypes.jl*
| The runtime form: a receipt binding a call to its standpoint (tool, version,
  database, release, parameters), its warrant (the classifier's numbers and
  the sample gates) and its projection (taxon, rank, feature, sample), sealed
  with a digest; and a merge verdict for two runs.
| Receipts carried by Protoctist rows; the manifest and pooling entries.
|===

[NOTE]
.A word used twice
====
_Occupancy_ in this catalogue means site-occupancy modelling in ecology:
whether a taxon occupies a site given imperfect detection. The estate's
*occupancy-types* repository is about memory occupancy of programs. It is not
used here, and the shared word is a coincidence.
====

[[tropes]]
== Tropes: a ledger of what each step loses

Trope particularity is a small fixed vocabulary for what happens to a specific
property as it passes through a system, and whether what survives is enough
for the use at hand. Its checker takes a graph of steps, each graded by what
it does to a property, composes the grades, and returns `p-sufficient` or
`p-insufficient` for a declared use, with the edge that caused the failure.

An amplicon pipeline is such a graph, and its common failures are losses that
nobody recorded. The proposal is that every Protoctist operation declares its
grade, so that a run can be checked against what an analysis needs.

[cols="2,2,4",options="header"]
|===
| Effect | Grade | Where it happens in a protist workflow

| `p-preserving`
| `Present`
| Reading a table without altering it; carrying a receipt.

| `p-projecting`
| `Dropped`
| Truncating a lineage to a rank: everything below is withheld, and the path
  to recover it is known. Keeping only the best placement per query.

| `p-collapsing`
| `Predicated(P)`
| Turning counts into presence or absence. Reporting "assigned at genus"
  instead of the lineage. What survives is the answer to one question.

| `p-attenuating`
| `Attenuated(δ)`
| Rarefying to a depth. Rounding. Summarising a placement distribution by its
  expected position.

| `p-fusing`
| `Fused(τ)`
| Clustering sequences into an OTU. Pooling samples. Rolling counts up a
  clade.

| `p-detaching`
| `Severed`
| Exporting a tree without the table it annotates. Dropping the sample
  metadata from a count matrix.

| `p-falsifying`
| `Falsified`
| Reading a blank count as zero. A deceptive effect: the result claims less
  loss than occurred. Protoctist refuses, with the row named.

| `p-misbinding`
| `Misbound`
| Reading a SILVA lineage against the PR2 ladder, so that a name sits at the
  wrong rank. Deceptive. Protoctist types every path by its ladder and never
  compares across them.

| `p-conflating`
| `Conflated`
| Two different nodes exported under one label, as in an iTOL dataset keyed by
  name when two taxa share a name. Deceptive. Detecting and refusing this is
  entry `PC-N02`.
|===

The grades are ordered
stem:[\mathsf{Falsified} \sqsubset \mathsf{Dropped} \sqsubset \mathsf{Predicated}(P) \sqsubset \mathsf{Attenuated}(\top) \sqsubset \cdots \sqsubset \mathsf{Attenuated}(1) \sqsubset \mathsf{Present}],
and compose along a path. An analysis declares a floor. Faith's phylogenetic
diversity needs branch lengths `Present`; computed on a taxonomy tree, where
they were never there, the verdict is `p-insufficient` and the witness is the
tree-building step. The user is told which step to change.

The three deceptive effects are the ones Protoctist's existing design refuses
by construction. That is not a coincidence of vocabulary: the calculus
separates honest loss, which composes, from deception, which is a validation
fault and cannot be written.

Two more results are measured, not proved, and are used as such.

* *Exact quality filtering.* An expected-error sum is an element of
  stem:[\mathbb{Q}(\theta)] with stem:[\theta = 10^{-1/10}], so comparison
  with a rational threshold is decidable exactly. On 152,360 real reads the
  exact and double-precision filters agreed on every read; on reads
  constructed to sum to exactly 2, double precision was wrong on all 2,000
  (MetaManifold-WebUI, `docs/cladistics/issue-303`, experiments 1 and 4).
* *Placement verdicts.* With the application's own placement step, clade
  verdicts from an amplicon fragment were right for 2,375 of 2,388 entailed
  cases, with no error among 1,515 verdicts on clades of 50 or more
  references and 7 errors among 62 on clades of two or three (the same
  document, experiment 8). Entry `PC-E05` builds on this.

== The atlas and the claims registry

Mathesis keeps an atlas of the mathematics across these repositories and a
registry of claim pins. A pin points at one theorem in its home repository, at
a commit and a receipt digest, and its grade is derived from the receipt,
never typed by hand.

The proposal for Protoctist is to treat <<obligations>> the same way. An
obligation enters this catalogue as [.st-open]#OPEN#. When its home repository
proves it and records a receipt, a claim pin is added in Mathesis, and the
catalogue entry cites the pin. Protoctist never asserts a grade for itself.

Two candidate bridges are worth recording for assessment. Neither is claimed.

* *Tree metrics and tropical geometry.* A dissimilarity is a tree metric
  exactly when it satisfies the four-point condition, which is the tropical
  Plücker relation; the space of tree metrics is a tropical Grassmannian
  (Speyer and Sturmfels 2004). The estate has a tropical-types development
  that this catalogue has not read. Whether its max-plus structures give a
  type for "this matrix is tree-like to within ε" is an open question, and
  the right place to ask it is Mathesis.
* *Zoom laws for placements.* MetaManifold-Evidence states, in a module not
  yet merged there, that an entailed verdict survives zooming out to an
  enclosing clade, and a refuted verdict survives zooming in, each under a
  premise on the error bounds. These are the laws a placement viewer needs
  (`PC-E06`).
