[[catalogue]]
= The catalogue: references, reads and assignment

[.lead]
Each entry gives the method, its defining formula, the proposed decision, the
repository that would own any new code, what the formal work adds, and the
obligation that must hold. Whether a Julia package for it exists and was
tested is in <<ecosystem>>; the obligations are collected in <<obligations>>.

== A. Lineages, ladders and reference frames

=== PC-A01 · Ladder-typed lineages

[horizontal]
What:: A lineage is a sequence of labels read against a named rank ladder. PR2 version 5 has nine ranks (domain, supergroup, division, subdivision, class, order, family, genus, species). Version 4 had eight; version 5.0.0 renamed kingdom to domain and inserted subdivision.
Definition:: A path is stem:[p = (\ell, a_1,\dots,a_k)] with ladder stem:[\ell] and stem:[k \le |\ell|]; stem:[a_i = \varnothing] marks an unfilled rank. Equality requires equal ladders.
Decision:: [.st-core]#CORE#. Exists in 0.1.0 for `:pr2` and `:silva`.
Proposed:: Ladders as data, with a version: `:pr2v4`, `:pr2v5`, `:silva138`, `:silva144`, `:eukaryome`. A ladder is declared, never inferred from the number of fields.
Estate:: Misreading a ladder is `p-misbinding`, a deceptive effect; the type prevents it.
Obligation:: PO-02.

=== PC-A02 · Reference database registry

[horizontal]
What:: A record for each reference: name, release, date, ladder, licence, size, digest, and source URL. PR2 5.1.1; SILVA 144 (26 August 2026; its eukaryote sequences are included but stated by SILVA to be uncurated); EukRibo 2.0; EUKARYOME 2.1; UNITE 10.0; MIDORI2; GTDB R232 for prokaryote contaminants.
Decision:: [.st-core]#CORE# for the registry and digest check. Fetching is an explicit command; nothing downloads at load time.
Estate:: The registry entry is the `source`, `version` and `ladder` of a standpoint. Two runs against different releases are not poolable without a recorded reason (PC-M02).
Obligation:: PO-07.

=== PC-A03 · Frameworks without a fixed ladder

[horizontal]
What:: EukRibo and UniEuk give a taxonomy string of variable depth with supergroup and two taxogroup fields, not a fixed rank ladder. Eukaryote classification follows Adl et al. 2019 and Burki et al. 2020; no successor revision was found as of November 2026.
Decision:: [.st-core]#CORE#: a `:free` ladder kind whose paths compare by prefix only and have no `at_rank`.
Estate:: Asking a free-ladder path for a rank fails by name. Padding it to a ladder would be `p-falsifying`.
Obligation:: PO-02.

=== PC-A04 · Cross-ladder comparison

[horizontal]
What:: Comparing a PR2 lineage with a SILVA one.
Decision:: [.st-hold]#HOLD#. Protoctist refuses to mix ladders, and its canon says it is not a PR2-to-SILVA mapper.
If the review asks:: A crosswalk would be a partial map between ladders with an explicit grade per rank (`p-projecting` where a rank has no counterpart) and would live outside the core. It needs a curated table from specialists, which is why it is a question for BIOCEV and not a default.

=== PC-A05 · Classifier conventions

[horizontal]
What:: QIIME 2 rank prefixes (`d__`, `p__`), DADA2 `NA`, trailing separators, `_X` padding in PR2 for unnamed intermediate ranks, `incertae sedis`.
Decision:: [.st-core]#CORE#. `NA` and trailing separators are handled in 0.1.0; prefixes and `_X` padding are on the roadmap as options at load time.
Estate:: Each normalisation is a declared `p-attenuating` step; the original string is kept so the map is recoverable (echo).
Obligation:: PO-03.

== B. Reads and sequence processing

Protoctist does not process reads today. These entries decide who should.

=== PC-B01 · Sequence and quality I/O

[horizontal]
What:: FASTA, FASTQ, alignments.
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT# BioSequences, FASTX and BioAlignments from BioJulia.
Owner:: Extension `ProtoctistBioSequencesExt`: content-addressed feature identifiers and sequence columns in a `ProtistRun`.

=== PC-B02 · Expected-error filtering, exact

[horizontal]
Formula:: stem:[\mathrm{EE} = \sum_i 10^{-Q_i/10}], and a read passes if stem:[\mathrm{EE} \le \tau].
Exact form:: With stem:[\theta = 10^{-1/10}], a root of the irreducible stem:[10x^{10}-1], stem:[\mathrm{EE} = \sum_{r=0}^{9} c_r \theta^{r}] with rational stem:[c_r]. Comparison with a rational stem:[\tau] is decided exactly from integer counts per quality value.
Decision:: [.st-build]#BUILD#, small. Better in one respect: the decision is exact at the threshold, where double precision is measurably wrong on constructed reads and order-dependent on 98.1% of real ones.
Owner:: The read-processing repository if created; otherwise an optional module beside PC-B01.
Estate:: An exact comparison may be `FACTIVE`; a floating one is `BELIEF`.
Obligation:: PO-17. [.st-tested]#TESTED# in MetaManifold-WebUI issue 303; not proved.

=== PC-B03 · Primer and adapter trimming

[horizontal]
What:: Semi-global alignment of primers with an error rate; cutadapt is the reference. Primer sets come from pr2-primers; V4 with TAReuk454FWD1 and TAReukREV3, V9 with 1389F and 1510R.
Decision:: [.st-wrap]#WRAP# cutadapt (MIT) as the pipeline already does; [.st-core]#CORE# for a primer registry keyed to PC-A02.

=== PC-B04 · Denoising to amplicon sequence variants

[horizontal]
What:: DADA2 learns a per-run error model and partitions reads into exact variants.
Formula:: For sequence stem:[i] with abundance stem:[a_i] and a candidate parent stem:[j] with abundance stem:[n_j], the error rate is stem:[\lambda_{ji} = \prod_{l} p(j(l) \to i(l), q_i(l))] and the abundance p-value is stem:[p_A = \frac{1}{1-\rho_0}\sum_{a \ge a_i} \rho_a], with stem:[\rho_a] the Poisson density of mean stem:[n_j\lambda_{ji}]. A new partition is formed when stem:[p_A] falls below stem:[\Omega_A].
Decision:: [.st-wrap]#WRAP# through R for now, which is what MetaManifold does. [.st-hold]#HOLD# on a native Julia denoiser until BIOCEV marks it `now`.
Why hold:: A native denoiser is the largest single piece of work in this catalogue and must reproduce DADA2 on real runs before it is offered. Removing the R dependency is the gain.

=== PC-B05 · Clustering to operational units

[horizontal]
What:: swarm: single linkage with local threshold stem:[d = 1], broken at abundance valleys. VSEARCH: greedy centroid clustering at an identity threshold. UNOISE: abundance-skew denoising.
Decision:: [.st-wrap]#WRAP# swarm (AGPL-3.0) and VSEARCH (GPL-3.0 or BSD-2).
Estate:: Clustering is `p-fusing`. The identity threshold belongs in the manifest; a 97% run and a 99% run are refused by `validate_merge` today.

=== PC-B06 · Chimera detection

[horizontal]
What:: UCHIME scores a query against two parents by three-way alignment; DADA2 tests whether a variant is an exact bimera of more abundant ones.
Decision:: [.st-wrap]#WRAP# VSEARCH; DADA2's test with PC-B04.

=== PC-B07 · Copy number and intragenomic variation

[horizontal]
What:: rDNA copy number varies by orders of magnitude across protists and scales with cell size, so read share is not cell share. Intragenomic variants inflate variant counts.
Decision:: [.st-hold]#HOLD# on correction. [.st-core]#CORE# for a visible caveat: an abundance figure states that it counts reads.
Estate:: Reads to cells is a claim about a latent quantity. Without a bound on copy number it is `UNRESOLVED` (residual evidence): presence is established, quantity is not.

== C. Taxonomic assignment

Protoctist consumes assignments. It does not make them.

=== PC-C01 · Naive Bayesian classification

[horizontal]
Formula:: For a query with word set stem:[W] (8-mers) and genus stem:[G] with stem:[M] training sequences of which stem:[m(w)] contain word stem:[w], and stem:[P_w = \frac{n(w) + 1/2}{N + 1}] over all stem:[N] training sequences: stem:[P(w \mid G) = \frac{m(w) + P_w}{M + 1}] and stem:[\log P(S \mid G) = \sum_{w \in W} \log P(w \mid G)]. Confidence is the share of 100 bootstrap replicates, each on one eighth of the words, that return the same taxon.
Decision:: [.st-wrap]#WRAP# DADA2 `assignTaxonomy`, IDTAXA or VSEARCH SINTAX. Reading their output is in 0.1.0.
Estate:: The bootstrap value is the warrant's score; the threshold is the policy. Below threshold at a rank the row is `COLLAPSED` to the deepest rank that passes, and what lies below is the residual.
Obligation:: PO-19.

=== PC-C02 · Alignment-based assignment

[horizontal]
What:: Global alignment against a reference at an identity threshold; lowest common ancestor of the hits within a margin.
Formula:: stem:[\mathrm{LCA}(H) = \text{longest common prefix of } \{p_h : h \in H\}], with stem:[H] the hits within stem:[\delta] of the best identity.
Decision:: [.st-wrap]#WRAP# VSEARCH for the alignment; [.st-core]#CORE# for LCA over typed paths.
Estate:: LCA is `p-projecting` with a known residual: the number of named candidates below the LCA is exactly `LineageResidual.named_candidates`.
Obligation:: PO-02, PO-19.

=== PC-C03 · Placement-based assignment

[horizontal]
What:: Assign each query the taxonomy of the clades its placement mass falls in. See PC-E04.
Decision:: [.st-core]#CORE#, built on PC-E.

=== PC-C04 · Agreement between classifiers

[horizontal]
What:: Two classifiers, or two references, on the same features.
Formula:: Per rank stem:[r], agreement stem:[A_r = \frac{|\{f : a_r(f) = b_r(f) \ne \varnothing\}|}{|\{f : a_r(f) \ne \varnothing \wedge b_r(f) \ne \varnothing\}|}], reported with the three other cells (only one assigned, neither assigned) and never as a single number.
Decision:: [.st-core]#CORE#, same ladder only.
Estate:: Disagreement at a rank is a residual with two named candidates.

=== PC-C05 · Novel-lineage admissibility

[horizontal]
What:: A feature that classifies confidently to a deep rank and to nothing below it may be a novel lineage or an artefact.
Decision:: [.st-core]#CORE# for the record; no detector is proposed.
Estate:: `LineageResidual.novel_ok` is set only after the contamination and chimera gates pass, as in EpistemicTypes.jl. "Novel" is never a default reading of "unassigned".
Obligation:: PO-19.
