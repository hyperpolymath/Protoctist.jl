[[obligations]]
= Proof obligations

== How to read the register

[.lead]
An obligation is a statement that must be true for a catalogue entry to be
trusted. This register says what each one is, how far it has got, and which
prover would check it.

The statements below were drafted for this catalogue. They are proposals for
formal statements, written in ordinary mathematical language, and they are
provisional until a proof assistant accepts a formal version. Where a row says
[.st-tested]#TESTED#, a program generated cases and checked them: the script
is `docs/catalogue/evidence/obligation_checks.jl`, its output is beside it, and
it ran under Julia 1.13.4 on 18 November 2026. A test that passes on two
thousand cases is not a proof.

Proof work follows the estate's policy: Agda under
`--safe --without-K` as the source of truth for theory, with Rocq and Lean
legs owed for a full grade; statements about one function of one repository
are engineering and carry a status only.

== The register

[cols="1,5,2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Id | Statement | Status | Route

| PO-01
| *Rollup conserves.* For any tree and any rows, the own counts sum to the
  row total and the root's cumulative count equals it, including rows whose
  lineage is absent from the tree and are counted at their deepest ancestor
  present. A child's cumulative count never exceeds its parent's.
| [.st-tested]#TESTED# 1,000 of 1,000. The tip-count case is
  [.st-proven]#PROVEN# in MetaManifold-WebUI.
| Agda, extending `CladeCumulus` with the attribution map. Engineering leg:
  property test in this repository.

| PO-02
| *Ladders are respected.* Paths on different ladders are never equal;
  truncation to a rank is idempotent and monotone in the rank; the common
  prefix of two paths is a path on the same ladder.
| [.st-open]#OPEN#. Covered by unit tests, not by a property test.
| Agda, small. A ladder-indexed vector type makes the first clause hold by
  construction.

| PO-03
| *Lineage round trip.* Parsing a lineage string and printing it returns the
  string up to the documented normalisations (trailing separators), and the
  original is recoverable from what is kept.
| [.st-open]#OPEN#
| Agda over `echo-types`: the normalisation as a retention with a stated
  residue.

| PO-04
| *Placement round trip.* Writing a batch and reading it back returns the same
  placements, with `NaN` for `null` both ways. Stated loss today: `nm`
  multiplicities and the metadata block.
| [.st-open]#OPEN#. Unit-tested for the fields kept.
| Engineering. Property test over generated batches; then remove the loss.

| PO-05
| *Placement mass.* For each query the weights of its candidates sum to at
  most one, and the mass on a clade is at most the mass on any clade
  containing it. The zoom laws of PC-E06 hold under their stated premises.
| [.st-open]#OPEN#. The sum was checked only on generated input. The zoom laws
  are stated in an unmerged module of MetaManifold-Evidence.
| Agda in MetaManifold-Evidence for the zoom laws. The sum is a property of
  the placer's output and is checked at read time, with a tolerance.

| PO-06
| *Selection ignores order.* The best placement of a query is a maximum of its
  weights and does not depend on the order in which candidates are listed.
| [.st-tested]#TESTED# 500 of 500.
| Engineering. Property test.

| PO-07
| *Merge verdict.* Reflexive (a manifest merges with itself), symmetric in its
  outcome, and complete: every field of the judgement surface that differs
  appears among the reasons.
| [.st-tested]#TESTED# for reflexivity and symmetry, 1,000 of 1,000.
  Completeness [.st-open]#OPEN#.
| Agda, small, as a decision procedure with a proof that the reason list is
  empty iff the surfaces are equal.

| PO-08
| *Exact neighbour joining.*
  (a) The row bound: every unexamined entry of a sorted row has
  stem:[Q \ge q_{\text{bound}}].
  (b) Inserting a new row keeps the row sorted and the running maximum valid.
  (c) A search pruned by (a) selects the same pair as the full scan under the
  same tie rule.
  (d) With the tie rule of PC-D05 over a content-derived order, the tree is a
  function of the set of sequences.
  (e) The chunk bound of PC-D07 is a lower bound in exact arithmetic.
  (f) A floating comparison is accepted only with an exact recheck or a proved
  error bound.
| (a) [.st-proven]#PROVEN# in `epistemic-types` for any ordered group.
  (c) [.st-tested]#TESTED# 300 of 300 runs, 286,709 entries skipped, no
  violation. (b), (d), (e), (f) [.st-open]#OPEN#. The need for (d) and (f)
  is itself tested: see PC-D05.
| Agda in `epistemic-types`: (b) is the missing invariant maintenance, (e) is
  `qBound-≤-Q` with a per-chunk maximum, and a rational instance closes the
  gap to p-distances. (d) in Agda over finite sets. (f) by an error-bound
  lemma, or avoided by rational arithmetic.

| PO-09
| *Distance domains.* Each corrected distance is defined exactly when its
  logarithms' arguments are positive, is zero iff the sequences agree on the
  compared sites, and is increasing in the observed difference.
| [.st-open]#OPEN#
| SMT for the domain conditions over the rationals; Agda or Lean for
  monotonicity.

| PO-10
| *Hill numbers.* Non-increasing in the order stem:[q]; order zero is
  richness; every order lies between one and richness.
| [.st-tested]#TESTED# 2,000 of 2,000 in floating point.
  [.st-assumed]#ASSUMED# from Hill (1973).
| Lean with Mathlib, as a consequence of power-mean monotonicity.

| PO-11
| *UniFrac.* Unweighted and normalised weighted UniFrac lie in stem:[[0,1]],
  are symmetric, and are zero for identical samples.
| Numerator at most denominator [.st-proven]#PROVEN# in MetaManifold-WebUI.
  The rest [.st-open]#OPEN#.
| Agda, extending `CladeCumulus`.

| PO-13
| *Centred log-ratio.* Sums to zero; invariant under rescaling; every path
  states its zero policy.
| Identities [.st-proven]#PROVEN# in CompositionalDA.jl.
  [.st-tested]#TESTED# here 2,000 of 2,000.
| Done in its home repository.

| PO-14
| *Aitchison distance.* Invariant under rescaling either sample; the distance
  on a subcomposition never exceeds the distance on the whole.
| [.st-tested]#TESTED# 2,000 of 2,000 each. [.st-assumed]#ASSUMED# from
  Aitchison (1982).
| Lean with Mathlib.

| PO-15
| *Benjamini–Hochberg.* Adjusted values dominate the raw ones, are at most
  one, and preserve order.
| [.st-proven]#PROVEN# in MetaManifold-WebUI and CompositionalDA.jl.
  [.st-tested]#TESTED# here on exact rationals.
| Done.

| PO-16
| *Permutation tests.* The p-value is at least stem:[1/(P+1)]; a term whose
  labels are fixed within every block is untestable.
| [.st-proven]#PROVEN# in MetaManifold-WebUI.
| Done. Open: that the pseudo-F statistic is non-negative for a Euclidean
  embedding.

| PO-17
| *Exact quality filter.* Comparison of an expected-error sum with a rational
  threshold is decidable from integer counts per quality value.
| [.st-tested]#TESTED# in MetaManifold-WebUI issue 303 against 512-bit
  arithmetic, 60,000 comparisons.
| Lean: irreducibility of stem:[10x^{10} - 1] and a sign decision in the
  number field. Or SMT over algebraic numbers.

| PO-19
| *Status is never raised.* Factive and belief counts at a node sum to at most
  its cumulative count; the factive count of a clade is exactly the sum of the
  factive rows beneath it. "Novel" is reachable only after the contamination
  gates pass.
| [.st-tested]#TESTED# 1,000 of 1,000 for the counts. The gate ordering is
  [.st-open]#OPEN# here and is implemented in EpistemicTypes.jl.
| Agda in `epistemic-types`, as a homomorphism from rows to nodes that has no
  map from belief to factive.

| PO-21
| *Richness estimators.* Chao1 is at least the observed richness; coverage
  lies in stem:[[0,1]].
| [.st-tested]#TESTED# 2,000 of 2,000 on exact rationals.
| SMT.

| PO-22
| *Placement spread.* The expected distance between placement locations is
  non-negative and is zero iff all mass is at one point.
| [.st-open]#OPEN#
| Agda or Lean, small.

| PO-23
| *Rarefaction.* Expected richness is non-decreasing in depth and equals the
  observed richness at full depth.
| [.st-tested]#TESTED# 2,000 of 2,000 on exact rationals.
| Lean with Mathlib.

| PO-25
| *No deceptive export.* Every export path has a declared trope grade, and an
  export that would put two nodes under one label is refused.
| [.st-open]#OPEN#. Not implemented.
| The trope checker, once Protoctist emits its intermediate representation.

== What the tests found

Three results from the script are worth a reviewer's attention, because they
are about amplicon-length data and none is flattering to common practice.

* *Ties are the normal case.* Every one of 300 generated sets of 240-site
  sequences produced at least one tied join.
* *Input order changes the tree.* With ties broken by position, as most
  programs do, shuffling the input changed the tree in 61 of 300 runs. The
  arithmetic was exact, so this is not rounding.
* *Rounding changes the tree.* Double precision and exact rational arithmetic
  gave different trees in 34 of 300 runs on the same input order.

The generated data are not real reads, and the proportions will differ on real
runs. Experiment E-3 repeats this on data BIOCEV chooses.

== Routes, in order of expected value

. *Row insertion for RapidNJ* (PO-08b) and a *rational instance*, in
  `epistemic-types`. These turn the existing proof from a rule about one row
  into a statement about a whole run on p-distances.
. *Rollup with attribution* (PO-01) and *status homomorphism* (PO-19). These
  are the two properties every Protoctist figure depends on.
. *Tie rule* (PO-08d). Small, and it is what makes "the neighbour-joining
  tree" a well-defined object.
. *Zoom laws* (PO-05), by merging the module that already states them.
. *The community formulae* (PO-10, 14, 23) in Lean, where the analysis library
  makes them short.
