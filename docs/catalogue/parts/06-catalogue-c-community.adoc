= The catalogue: communities, detection and reporting

Notation: a sample has counts stem:[n_1,\dots,n_S] with total stem:[N] and
proportions stem:[p_i = n_i/N]; stem:[f_k] is the number of taxa seen exactly
stem:[k] times.

== G. Diversity within a sample

=== PC-G01 · Richness, Shannon, Simpson

[horizontal]
Formulae:: stem:[S_{\text{obs}} = |\{i : n_i > 0\}|]; stem:[H = -\sum_i p_i \ln p_i]; stem:[\lambda = \sum_i p_i^2], Gini–Simpson stem:[1-\lambda].
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT#, with an exact path: richness and proportions as integers and rationals, as MetaManifold's approved method catalogue does.
Estate:: Descriptive only. No interval or test is implied.

=== PC-G02 · Hill numbers

[horizontal]
Formula:: stem:[{}^qD = \left(\sum_i p_i^{\,q}\right)^{1/(1-q)}] for stem:[q \ne 1], and stem:[{}^1D = \exp(H)]. stem:[{}^0D = S_{\text{obs}}], stem:[{}^2D = 1/\lambda] (Hill 1973; Jost 2006).
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT#.
Obligation:: PO-10.

=== PC-G03 · Richness estimators

[horizontal]
Chao1:: stem:[\hat S = S_{\text{obs}} + \frac{f_1^2}{2 f_2}], and stem:[S_{\text{obs}} + \frac{f_1(f_1-1)}{2}] when stem:[f_2 = 0].
Good's coverage:: stem:[\hat C = 1 - f_1/N].
Caution:: Denoisers remove or alter singletons, so stem:[f_1] after DADA2 is not a sampling quantity. The estimator is refused on a table marked as denoised unless the user overrides by name.
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT#, behind that gate.
Obligation:: PO-21.

=== PC-G04 · Rarefaction and extrapolation

[horizontal]
Formula:: Expected richness in a subsample of size stem:[m]: stem:[E[S_m] = \sum_i \left[1 - \binom{N - n_i}{m}\Big/\binom{N}{m}\right]].
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT#. Whether to rarefy is the analyst's call; both sides of the debate are cited (McMurdie and Holmes 2014; Schloss 2024).
Estate:: Rarefying is `p-attenuating`. The depth and the seed go in the manifest.
Obligation:: PO-23.

=== PC-G05 · Faith's phylogenetic diversity

[horizontal]
Formula:: stem:[\mathrm{PD}(X) = \sum_{e \in T(X)} \ell_e], the total length of the edges spanning the taxa stem:[X] and the root.
Decision:: [.st-core]#CORE# through the Cladistics extension.
Proved elsewhere:: [.st-proven]#PROVEN# monotone and bounded (`faithPD-mono`, `faithPD-bounded`).
Estate:: Needs branch lengths `Present`. On a taxonomy tree the request is `p-insufficient` and is refused.

=== PC-G06 · Phylogenetic Hill numbers

[horizontal]
Formula:: With stem:[a_e] the relative abundance descending from edge stem:[e] and stem:[\bar T = \sum_e \ell_e a_e]: stem:[{}^q\bar D(\bar T) = \left[\sum_e \frac{\ell_e}{\bar T} a_e^{\,q}\right]^{1/(1-q)}] (Chao, Chiu and Jost 2010).
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT# if Diversity.jl with Phylo.jl passes the survey; otherwise [.st-build]#BUILD# on PC-F04.

== H. Differences between samples

=== PC-H01 · Abundance and incidence dissimilarities

[horizontal]
Bray–Curtis:: stem:[\mathrm{BC}(x,y) = \frac{\sum_i |x_i - y_i|}{\sum_i (x_i + y_i)}].
Jaccard:: stem:[J(A,B) = 1 - \frac{|A\cap B|}{|A\cup B|}].
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT# Distances.jl.
Estate:: Jaccard is `p-collapsing`: it sees presence only.

=== PC-H02 · UniFrac

[horizontal]
Unweighted:: stem:[U = \frac{\sum_e \ell_e\,|\mathbb{1}[a_e>0] - \mathbb{1}[b_e>0]|}{\sum_e \ell_e\,\max(\mathbb{1}[a_e>0], \mathbb{1}[b_e>0])}].
Weighted:: stem:[W = \sum_e \ell_e\,|a_e - b_e|], normalised by stem:[\sum_i d_i (a_i + b_i)] with stem:[d_i] the root-to-tip distance.
Generalised:: stem:[\frac{\sum_e \ell_e (a_e + b_e)^{\alpha}\left|\frac{a_e - b_e}{a_e + b_e}\right|}{\sum_e \ell_e (a_e + b_e)^{\alpha}}] (Chen et al. 2012).
Decision:: [.st-build]#BUILD# on PC-F04. No registered Julia implementation was found; the estate has one inside MetaManifold, checked against scikit-bio.
Proved elsewhere:: [.st-proven]#PROVEN# that the unweighted numerator never exceeds the denominator.
Obligation:: PO-11.

=== PC-H03 · Aitchison distance

[horizontal]
Formula:: stem:[d_A(x,y) = \lVert \mathrm{clr}(x) - \mathrm{clr}(y) \rVert_2], with stem:[\mathrm{clr}(x)_i = \ln x_i - \tfrac1S\sum_j \ln x_j].
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT# CompositionalDA.jl for the transform; zero policy explicit (PC-I02).
Obligation:: PO-14.

=== PC-H04 · Ordination

[horizontal]
Principal coordinates:: From stem:[D], form stem:[B = -\tfrac12 J D^{(2)} J] with stem:[J = I - \tfrac1n \mathbf{1}\mathbf{1}^{\!\top}], and take stem:[X = V_k \Lambda_k^{1/2}] from its leading eigenpairs.
Non-metric scaling:: Minimise stress stem:[\sqrt{\sum (d_{ij} - \hat d_{ij})^2 / \sum d_{ij}^2}] over configurations.
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT# MultivariateStats.jl.
Estate:: Negative eigenvalues are reported with their share, not discarded silently.

=== PC-H05 · Permutational analysis of variance

[horizontal]
Formula:: stem:[F = \frac{SS_A/(a-1)}{SS_W/(N-a)}], with stem:[SS_T = \frac1N\sum_{i<j} d_{ij}^2] and stem:[SS_W = \sum_g \frac{1}{n_g}\sum_{i<j \in g} d_{ij}^2] (Anderson 2001). The p-value is stem:[\frac{1 + \#\{F^\pi \ge F\}}{1 + P}] over stem:[P] permutations.
Decision:: [.st-build]#BUILD# in Julia, with PERMDISP beside it. No registered Julia package was found; MetaManifold currently calls R.
Proved elsewhere:: [.st-proven]#PROVEN# that the smallest reachable p-value is stem:[1/(P+1)], and which terms are untestable because their labels are fixed within every block.
Estate:: A p-value below its floor is impossible and is refused; a term whose labels never exchange has its p-value withheld. Exchangeability is an assumption the user states, not one a seed supplies.
Obligation:: PO-16.

== I. Composition and differential abundance

=== PC-I01 · Scaling

[horizontal]
Total sum:: stem:[p_i = n_i/N], with stem:[\sum_i p_i = 1].
Median of ratios:: stem:[s_j = \operatorname{median}_i \frac{n_{ij}}{(\prod_k n_{ik})^{1/m}}] over taxa with no zero.
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT# CompositionalDA.jl.
Proved elsewhere:: [.st-proven]#PROVEN# sums and bounds (`Scaling`).

=== PC-I02 · Log-ratio transforms and zeros

[horizontal]
Transforms:: Centred, additive stem:[\mathrm{alr}(x)_i = \ln(x_i/x_S)] and isometric log-ratios.
Zero policies:: Refuse; pseudocount; multiplicative replacement stem:[r_i = \delta] if stem:[x_i = 0], else stem:[x_i\left(1 - \frac{\sum_{z}\delta}{c}\right)] for a composition summing to stem:[c] (Martín-Fernández et al. 2003).
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT# CompositionalDA.jl, where normalised input is refused by type and no path handles zeros silently.
Proved elsewhere:: [.st-proven]#PROVEN# algebraic identities of the centred log-ratio, each mirrored by a property test.
Obligation:: PO-13.

=== PC-I03 · Differential abundance

[horizontal]
ALDEx2:: Dirichlet Monte Carlo instances of each sample, centred log-ratio, then Welch and Wilcoxon tests averaged over instances.
ANCOM-BC:: stem:[\ln n_{ij} = d_j + \mathbf{x}_j^{\!\top}\boldsymbol\beta_i + \varepsilon_{ij}] with a sample bias stem:[d_j] estimated and removed (Lin and Peddada 2020).
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT# CompositionalDA.jl for ALDEx2. [.st-hold]#HOLD# on ANCOM-BC: that package's own calibration pilot found severe false-discovery inflation with four against sixteen samples under a complete null, and it makes no calibration claim.
Estate:: A result names its method, zero policy and number of Monte Carlo instances.

=== PC-I04 · Count models with excess zeros

[horizontal]
Negative binomial:: stem:[n_{ij} \sim \mathrm{NB}(\mu_{ij}, \phi_i)], stem:[\ln\mu_{ij} = \ln s_j + \mathbf{x}_j^{\!\top}\boldsymbol\beta_i].
Zero-inflated:: stem:[P(n = 0) = \pi + (1-\pi)\,\mathrm{NB}(0)], stem:[P(n = k) = (1-\pi)\,\mathrm{NB}(k)] for stem:[k>0].
Hurdle:: stem:[P(n = 0) = \pi_0], stem:[P(n = k) = (1-\pi_0)\frac{\mathrm{NB}(k)}{1 - \mathrm{NB}(0)}] for stem:[k>0].
Decision:: [.st-hold]#HOLD#. ZeroInflatedCounts.jl is a specification with no implementation yet.

=== PC-I05 · Multiple testing

[horizontal]
Benjamini–Hochberg:: For ordered stem:[p_{(1)} \le \dots \le p_{(m)}]: stem:[q_{(k)} = \min_{j \ge k} \min\!\left(1, \frac{m}{j} p_{(j)}\right)].
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT# CompositionalDA.jl `bh_adjust`.
Proved elsewhere:: [.st-proven]#PROVEN#: stem:[q \ge p], stem:[q \le 1], order preserved.

== J. Detection, contamination and occupancy

=== PC-J01 · Detection above a noise bound

[horizontal]
Rule:: With stem:[y] reads observed and noise at most stem:[n] in either direction, the candidate true counts are stem:[[\max(0, y-n),\, y+n]]. Present is entailed iff stem:[n < y]; refuted iff stem:[y = n = 0]; otherwise unresolved, with a witness each way.
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT# the rule; [.st-core]#CORE# for carrying the verdict on a node.
Proved elsewhere:: [.st-proven]#PROVEN# in MetaManifold-Evidence in Agda, Rocq and Lean.
Estate:: The noise bound is a declared assumption, typically from negative controls. Without one, every nonzero count is unresolved.

=== PC-J02 · Contaminant identification

[horizontal]
Frequency:: A contaminant's frequency falls as input DNA rises: compare the fits stem:[\ln f = \beta_0 - \ln c] and stem:[\ln f = \beta_0] (Davis et al. 2018).
Prevalence:: Compare presence in negative controls with presence in true samples by a stem:[2\times2] test.
Decision:: [.st-build]#BUILD#, small, in Julia. No Julia implementation was found.
Estate:: Contamination suspected sets `LineageResidual.contaminated`, which closes the route to "novel".

=== PC-J03 · Cross-talk between samples

[horizontal]
What:: Tag jumps and index hopping put a small share of an abundant taxon's reads into other samples (Schnell et al. 2015).
Decision:: [.st-core]#CORE# as a noise bound for PC-J01: stem:[n_{ij} = \lceil \rho \cdot \max_{j'} y_{ij'} \rceil] for a declared rate stem:[\rho].

=== PC-J04 · Site occupancy

[horizontal]
Model:: Site stem:[i] is occupied with probability stem:[\psi]; each of stem:[K] visits detects with probability stem:[p]. For a history with stem:[d] detections: stem:[L = \psi\, p^{d}(1-p)^{K-d} + (1-\psi)\,\mathbb{1}[d = 0]].
Decision:: [.st-hold]#HOLD#. MetaManifold has a low-level estimator that is not yet a workflow; it needs repeated visits and refuses single-visit histories.

== K. Associations and assembly

=== PC-K01 · Association networks

[horizontal]
What:: SparCC, SPIEC-EASI, and FlashWeave, which is written in Julia.
Decision:: [.st-adopt]#ADOPT# FlashWeave.jl (GPL-3.0) if it passes the survey.
Estate:: An edge is an association under a model, never an interaction.

=== PC-K02 · Phylogenetic community structure

[horizontal]
Formulae:: stem:[\mathrm{NRI} = -\frac{\mathrm{MPD}_{\text{obs}} - \overline{\mathrm{MPD}}_{\text{null}}}{\mathrm{sd}(\mathrm{MPD}_{\text{null}})}], and stem:[\beta\mathrm{NTI}] likewise from the between-sample mean nearest-taxon distance (Webb et al. 2002; Stegen et al. 2013).
Decision:: [.st-hold]#HOLD#. They need a stable phylogeny, which PC-D10 shows an amplicon tree is not.

== L. Protist-specific concerns

=== PC-L01 · Trophic mode and function

[horizontal]
What:: Trait tables keyed by taxon (Ramond et al. 2019; Schneider et al. 2020; the Mixoplankton Database, Mitra et al. 2023).
Decision:: [.st-core]#CORE# for a join by typed lineage at a stated rank, with the unmatched share reported.
Estate:: A trait inherited from a genus is `p-attenuating`; from a class it may be `p-insufficient` for a functional claim.

=== PC-L02 · Parasites and host signal

[horizontal]
What:: Syndiniales and apicomplexans can dominate read counts; host reads dominate gut and tissue samples.
Decision:: [.st-core]#CORE#: named category filters recorded in the manifest, so that "after removing host" is part of the judgement surface.

=== PC-L03 · Long-read ribosomal operons

[horizontal]
What:: Full 18S or 18S–28S reads give trees that short amplicons cannot (Jamy et al. 2020).
Decision:: [.st-core]#CORE# for formats; this is the case where PC-D becomes useful directly.

=== PC-L04 · Units of diversity

[horizontal]
What:: No single identity threshold delimits protist species; richness depends on the unit (Caron and Hu 2019).
Decision:: [.st-core]#CORE#: every richness figure states its unit and threshold.
